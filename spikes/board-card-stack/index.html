The stack card — firmware and hardware on every card

A quick experiment. The full device card’s three zones (project, firmware, device), shrunk onto the home card: the picture and the project row, then one row for the firmware and one for the hardware, each a one-line summary. Click a row for its details (a popover). The real data is one click away and grounds the card in what the board actually is. No boxes in boxes: a notice isn’t a pill on the picture any more. It tints the row of the layer it belongs to, edge to edge, sharing the card’s border, and its action sits flush at that row’s end. A fine board shows its frame rate. The page below uses the leans; the questions are lettered to match the chat. Click a picture or name to open; click a firmware or hardware row for details.
