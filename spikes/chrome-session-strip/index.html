Chrome session strip — what's running, and where you are

Thesis (vision follow-up, 2026-08-05): runtime sessions are global — the pool, sims, and device links keep running while you sit on Boards or Docs — but nothing outside the gallery page shows them, and the editor has no representation in the nav at all (today the Studio tab even detaches your lens when clicked). The strip makes each live session a place in the chrome: one chip per running session, name + status dot only (D43: wayfinding, never a device surface — no controls ever), the lensed one marked, click = open in the editor. Chip exists exactly while the session does (D36 rule); registry-offline devices never appear. Builds on chrome C (D11, split weights, ⋯ at narrow).

3 The return trip — a working miniature (concept A wiring)

The interaction the strip exists for. You're editing Porch sign; wander to Boards; the chip stays lit ("open"); click it and you're back in your editor, lens intact. Also shows the live-vs-registry rule: Garage matrix is registered but offline — it has a card on the devices page, and no chip. Everything here is clickable: brand, tabs, chips, cards.

Lean: A. Sessions are places you go, not telemetry — putting them with your-stuff makes the active chip read as "where I am", which is the whole point (and it's what makes deleting the Studio-tab-detaches-lens behavior safe). B keeps the nav pure but the chips read as status lights, and the right side is already contended (branch chip, AI, settings). C is nearly free and is the natural sm-collapse of A — but as the only affordance it hides the one thing we want visible, and gives the lensed session no presence at all. Narrow lean: count pill opening the flyout, accent-ringed when your editor is inside.

Deliberately absent, per D43 + small-scope: live thumbnails in chips (the global PreviewHost could feed them later — sparkle, not round one), any control on a chip (play/stop/eject), offline/registry devices in the strip, and any second sim.
