Project identity & sharing — design spike

Vision D1–D13 (2026-08-07-1630-project-identity-sharing): the address bar is the share link — every project lives at /p/<slug>-<uid>, auto-published, viewable by anyone with the link from birth; the Share popover is pure access control (restricted / view / edit ⊥ people); visitors get a real editor over a tracking copy with a two-state banner; removal is Archive, never bare delete. Uids wear PR #384's shape: prj + 16-char lowercase base-32, case-insensitive. Everything here reacts to the controls strip — flip access, people, and the visitor's divergence state. 5 · Archive — the removal verb

D8: no irreversible actions on the main surface. My lean (Q4 of the assumptions): archive lives in the project's ⋯ menu — the Share popover stays pure access control, like Docs. The archive page is where "Delete forever" is allowed to exist, spelled like what it is.
