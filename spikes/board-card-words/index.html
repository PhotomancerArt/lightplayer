Board card words

Read each row of the card on its own. It should say what it is about and what state it is in, without its icon and without the rows around it, to someone who has never seen Studio. Below: a few whole cards, then one section per row with every state that row can show. Each state has today’s words (from the connected branch, after your first round of notes) beside the words I propose, and why. The picture itself is left alone; we’ll talk about it separately.

Changed states have a blue ID and a change tag; unchanged ones are grey. Reply with IDs (“L2 yes, A2 no, keep it shorter”).
