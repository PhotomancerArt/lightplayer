3The project is the URL; ?on= says which device (D43)

Opening a project always needs a device and never touches silicon by itself (D33). ?on=emu and ?on=sim name a kind and resolve to the device of that kind for the project's target (the one that last ran it, else an idle one, else create); ?on=mac:… names a board by identity (silicon, or an emu by its synthetic MAC); ?on=ws:host:port names a device over WebSocket (a real desktop server, or a native emu backing). No ?on= → emu if a module exists, else sim. The opening frame narrates the device. Cycle Face.

Rejected, and why.
  • The twin verb ("Emulate this board") on a real card, in the device zone or the ⋯ menu. Yona: "not sure I understand why … I could imagine having a way to add an emulated device on the device page but that's all." D2's "emulate that actual board" is an emu of the same kind plus Push — two verbs that exist.
  • "Emulated" on the project's board rows (round 2's settings row). "emulated is something that a device is, a board is just a board."
  • Desktop as a device record, and the "costume" (round 2's D31/D34). Desktop is a target; a sim of a board is a sim, first-class, not Desktop in disguise. The band says Sim · XIAO ESP32-C6 and that is the whole truth.
  • Today's sim card as the "what leaves" reference (round 2, section 4 S). It was the OLD device-card anatomy (tint edge, title bar, icon tabs), not the current roster card; its removal is chipped as its own cleanup task.
  • Dress A (words only), B (violet left edge), C (kind chip), D (ghost frame) — round 1. The band says it; a second dress calls it out harder than needed.
  • The "Emulated" title prefix, the violet ▶ pill. The title is the board's or target's name; the pill reads live on every card (the frames are honestly that device's); the band is the one bound-family element.
  • 3B the package-card sheet, 3C the never-block banner, "twin standing by". There is no ask on open (D33).
  • The emulated board or the sim wearing a different anatomy (title bar + icon tabs). They are devices — the four zones, the same verbs, the same fold. There is no runtime that is not a device (D30).
  • A separate "Emulated"/"Simulated" section in the roster. One roster; grouping is a sort, later.
  • "Emulator" / "Simulator" as card titles. Reserved words for the things, not names for a device; the card names its target.
  • A fake board photo on the card. A product shot of hardware the user does not have — the opposite of T7.
  • The device in the address path (/device/<uid> as the lens address). The identity is the project; the device is a hint.
