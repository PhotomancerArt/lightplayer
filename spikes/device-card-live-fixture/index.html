Device card — show what's running on the board

G2 ruling: the hero strip's re-simulated raster reads dishonest on a device card ("too smooth to believe that's coming from the actual device"). Thesis: when a device is connected, the card should primarily show the running control product — the fixture's lamps, fed by actual frames off the link — degrading honestly (frame age, stalls) and falling back to a dimmed last-known frame offline. Sim cards may keep re-simulation: they are the simulator. A 3D fixture model may take this slot later; every concept below treats the lamp view as "the best honest thing we can render today".

Pattern Fixture Device fps Reality

Try "device hiccups": the board stalls ~700 ms every few seconds. Concept A keeps gliding — it can't know. Every honest concept freezes with the board and says how old the frame is. That contrast is the whole argument.

1 · Today (baseline) — re-simulated raster hero

Production gallery-rework P05: 44px hero strip under the title bar, re-simulating the project's visual raster in the browser. Smooth, pretty, and unrelated to what the silicon is doing right now.

2 · Honest hero — fixture strip under the title bar

Same slot, honest content: the strip renders the fixture's lamp layout with frames read back from the device. Keeps the "identity at a glance" role; the LIVE pill carries frame age. Offline = dimmed last-known frame (D12 stays). Sim = smooth, marked SIM.

3 · Play tab — the preview is a tab, not a banner

G2 direction (1) taken literally: no hero strip at all. A ▶ tab — the connected card's default tab — holds a taller fixture view plus a meta row (project · fps · frame age). Status stays one tab away. The card is shorter when you're on another tab.

4 · Status-face embed — health and output in one glance

No new tab: the Status face embeds a mid-height fixture view between the status headline and the detail rows. "Is it okay" and "what is it doing" answer together; the Play tab's extra click disappears, but Status gets denser.

5 · Wire ticker + Play tab — output-space honesty

A skinny per-wire ticker under the title bar: one row per output wire, each LED a pixel column — literally what the wires carry (zook: 5 × 300). Spatial fixture view lives in the ▶ tab. The ticker is cheap at any scale and reads like a VU meter; whether it reads as "the artwork" is the question.
