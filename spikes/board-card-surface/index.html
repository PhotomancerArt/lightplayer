The card’s top as its main surface

Yona, 2026-10-17: the primary surface should always give the most important information. The top of the board card, today its picture, is its main surface, and it is never black. And: the primary action slot should hold the most likely action a user wants to take, the button to the right of the board’s name, even when it repeats a row’s own button. The surface explains; the primary gives the thing to do. There are no buttons inside the surface.

What prompted it: the desk board is running an Oct 3 firmware that this Studio can’t talk to. Its card today is a black picture, a name and no primary, Project none (false: it is playing a project Studio can’t read), and a red Update on the fourth row. Yona: this board is connected by usb, but I can’t connect/edit it? feels like a dead end.
