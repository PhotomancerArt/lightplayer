LightPlayer logo — concept spike

Exploring the LightPlayer brand from the liked direction — a play triangle inside a WS2811-style addressable-LED package — plus motifs harvested from the Light at Play archive (spiral play, circuit-trace letterforms with via dots, rainbow-as-identity). §1 tunes the mark, §2 auditions wordmark typography, §3 auditions motion, and §4 is a live brand sheet: click a card in §1/§2/§3 and the sheet recomposes. Display faces load from Google Fonts (badged if offline); Inter & JetBrains Mono are the repo's vendored production fonts.
Gate-1 decisions (2026-08-03): rainbow wordmark = hover state in-app (concept F), loud D reserved for marketing surfaces; chip goes white + more technical (Chip tint/stroke controls, mark H); wordmark stays a basic sans — no need to get fancy. Gate-2: mark = H·Datasheet fine (confirmed), pin-1 dot dropped (accurate but noisy); rainbow scope D / D2 / D3 (“Player” still · triangle still) under judgment.

1 The mark — chip refinements & heritage hybrids

The production mark (first card) is the baseline. Chip variants keep the WS2811 package and tune what lives inside it; heritage variants remix the 2014 Light at Play spiral- and circuit-play motifs into (or out of) the package. Each card shows 40 / 22 / 16 px — the 22 px cell is the top-bar reality check. Click to feed §4.

2 The wordmark — typography audition

Each specimen: the wordmark large, then the full lockup at top-bar scale (22 px mark). Case and Word tone in the controls apply everywhere; “as designed” uses each face's preferred casing. The last three are custom letterform treatments, not fonts — the circuit-trace alphabet is redrawn from the 2014 wordmark (E and R are new glyphs in that style). Click to feed §4.
