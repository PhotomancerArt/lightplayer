Top bar & site chrome — design spike

Thesis: the top bar becomes real site navigation. LightPlayer is one cohesive app — Studio (the gallery + editor), Boards, and Docs are its sections. The logo is brand, not the home link (that's reserved for a future landing page); an explicit Studio nav item is the way home. The version indicator earns its place by showing the git branch on dev builds. The gear stops lying: it's AI settings.

1 Interim logo marks — circuit board × play

Two families: A–E put the play inside a circuit element; F–H invert it — the play button is the container and the circuit element is knocked out of it. Each shown at favicon (16), bar (22) and hero (56) sizes, plus the wordmark lockup. Click a card to use that mark in every bar below. All are single-color currentColor SVGs — they inherit the teal accent, recolor for free, and can ship in-repo like the board diagrams do.

A · Chip An IC package with the play triangle as its die. Reads "hardware product" instantly; very stable at 16px.

E · Pixel A WS2812 addressable-LED package — corner pads, lens, play in the lens. The most "LED" of the five; busiest at 16px.
