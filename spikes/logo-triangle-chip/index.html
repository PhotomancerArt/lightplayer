4 Hero — the shader triangle, three fills

Chosen direction: the triangle alone, no chip frame. The remaining question is the fill. Pure = continuous shader. Pixels = same shader through a fine LED-dot mask — solid silhouette at a glance, addressable pixels up close. Spill = pure fill plus a soft light bloom past the edges.
