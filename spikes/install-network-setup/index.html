Installing a board sets up its Wi‑Fi

Sean flashed his first board from lightplayer.app on 2026-10-18. Nothing asked for his Wi‑Fi, and he couldn’t find where to add it. His boards will light his house’s eaves and update over Wi‑Fi, and WLED, his reference, asks for Wi‑Fi right after a web flash. Yona, 2026-10-19: enabling wifi and ble should be part of the install step. ideally your account remembers wifi so its super easy in the future. this is our moment to get people to think about it.

The thesis: the install is the one moment everyone is at the board with a cable and their attention. Ask for Wi‑Fi there, from the networks the board hears, say once what the board can now be reached by, and let the account remember the network so the next board takes one press. Four ways to place it, beside today’s card. Press the buttons on each card to walk it; the strip sets who is signed in and how the join goes.

    4The account remembers networks
    
  
    What it looks like, and the security decision it needs. The account already keeps the board’s account keys and the optional play and edit passwords in the cloud, readable by the service (docs/adr/2026-09-24-easy-bluetooth-access.md, “Account keys and passwords live in the cloud”). That is the precedent. Storing a Wi‑Fi password there is its own decision, for an ADR in the plan that follows.
