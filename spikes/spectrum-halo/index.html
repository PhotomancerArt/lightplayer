§3 · settle on mouse-out — the angle must not snap under the fade

Hover, then leave, and watch the rainbow during the fade-out. The shipped halo (0) jumps to 0° the moment the pointer leaves. Each column changes only the mouse-out handling; the readout under each button is the live --ux-ir-angle of the halo and its opacity, so a mid-fade screenshot carries its own numbers. Script drivers, frame-free (the Browser pane's timeline is frozen while hidden): probeDet() pins every column, seeks the spins to 1.3s, releases, then seeks the fade-out transitions to fixed offsets and reads angle, opacity and each spin's play-state; freezeHover() / freezeDet(ms) park the row at hover or ms into the fade for a screenshot; thaw() resumes.
