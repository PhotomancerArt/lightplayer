2 Devices — the main jump-off

The old gallery's device roster becomes the whole page. The connect card splits: connect a device / simulate a device — same flow, one skips flash. Cards show what's running, immediately — toggle hero-strip vs header-chip in the controls (hover a running card: the strip animates, Shadertoy-style). The sim wears the same grammar in violet. Awkward cases included: long name, offline (dimmed last-known art), fresh board with nothing on it.

3 Creation wizard — board → content → (flash) → editor

Opens from either §2 card (or the controls strip). Hardware path: the probe suggests the detected board and adds a flash step. Sim path: any board, no flash — the sim inherits the board (vision D4). Content step: New shows the actual generated project for the chosen board; Load lists your projects — pick one targeting another board to see the mismatch warning (no auto-fixup, per D5). Finish to land in §4.
