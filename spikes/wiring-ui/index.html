Wiring UI — visualizing the bus

Decision log. Gate 1 (2026-08-03): horizontal flow is the direction; selection parked; node-graph later. Gate 2: value box confirmed at this small size; wires grew arrowheads to carry direction; nomenclature decided — D: panel (module) + settings (leaf), with the tint + ▶ play-surface treatment teaching the difference. Open: whether cross-scope readers list on the channel (the toggle below). Wire weight carries resolution — winning writer solid violet, shadowed dashed, E3 contention orange with the pick affordance. Hover highlights a node everywhere; clicking flashes it (focus-jump stand-in). Set-aside concepts are archived at the bottom.

1. The direction — [writers] → [value box] → [readers]

The channel box is a small value box: name in the violet header, what's on the channel as the body — a picture for visuals, a number + bar for floats, the authored default (italic) for R6 invitations, red for unresolved. Arrowheads on every wire carry direction: writers point into the box, the box points out to readers. The show inherited readers toggle: dotted chips like plasma_1 · sim (child scope) are consumers living in a child module whose read found no writer locally and walked out to this channel (R5) — they really are fed by it, but their binding lives in the child scope, not here. OFF summarizes them as "2 readers in child scopes" instead.

2. Nomenclature — decided at gate 2: panel + settings

PANEL is a module's performable product surface — tinted with the existing panel-primary token and wearing a ▶ play surface chip, because the panel is the thing play mode renders and that visual link is what teaches the module/leaf difference. A leaf's knob strip is its SETTINGS — you're configuring that one node. (Alternatives A and E are in the archive below.)
