4 Pin discovery — "which pin are my LEDs on?"



  We drive a distinct color × rhythm pattern on every free, output-capable pin.
  The user looks at their strip and clicks the pattern they see. Rhythm (solid /
  1-blink / 2-blink / chase) disambiguates for colorblind users; input-only and
  USB pins are skipped automatically.
