# The peach — sections of one strip wanting different looks

The stained-glass archetype: a physical artwork wired as **one LED
strip**, whose regions want **different patterns**. The regions are
index ranges of the same wire — nothing about the electronics
separates them.

The concrete peach: 2D artwork laser-etched onto acrylic with LEDs
in channels. One strip runs from the bottom of the peach body up
one side, across both leaves, and back down the other side. The
body (both legs) wants one look; the leaves want another.

Wire topology (from the reference art `lp-example-peach.svg`, whose
segment paths are authored wire-true):

```text
ch  0–21   body, leg 1 (bottom → top)
ch 22–33   leaves
ch 34–55   body, leg 2 (top → bottom — REVERSED relative to the
           body's own 1D pattern space, so gradients stay symmetric)
```

Real installs this abstracts: the OCF acrylic pieces (peach logo,
several similar works — leaves green, body pink), and in general
any segmented strip art (the whole WLED "segments" workflow).

## The two variants

Both are legitimate and both ship as examples:

- **peach-1d** — everything declared/consumed 1D; patterns run
  along the wire. This is how such art runs on WLED today.
- **peach-2d** — the same shape mapped in 2D; patterns are planes
  sampled at lamp positions. Idiomatic LightPlayer.

The patch — which lamps belong to which fixture, and where they
sit on the wire — is **identical between the variants**. That is
the point.

## Demands on the system

- **Multiple fixtures per output.** Body and leaves are separate
  fixtures (different patterns ⇒ different visual inputs) sharing
  one output. A fixture = a WLED segment, upgraded.
- **Fixture-relative range patching.** Each fixture claims ranges
  of the output's wire by channel offset; the body claims two
  discontiguous ranges; ranges can be `reversed`.
- **Dimension-agnostic patching.** The patch operates on wire
  ranges; whether the fixture's mapping is 1D or 2D must not
  change it.
- **Presentation independent of sampling.** The 1D variant still
  *draws* as a peach (the mapping doc is the presentation shape);
  sampling dimension comes from the product + consume policy, not
  from whether a drawing exists.
- **Overlap and gap diagnostics.** Mis-anchored ranges must render
  visibly wrong (contested lamps dark, status naming channels),
  never silently reorder or kill the frame.
