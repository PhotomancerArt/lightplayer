# Per-lane cost: projects and scripts

A **lane** is a fixture and the output it feeds. On the S3 the engine's
non-shader frame time fits `a + b × lanes + c × LEDs`, and the lane term
(`b`, ~1.85 ms on FS3 with the IRAM hot set) is what the engine frame-plan
work removes (planning `lp2025/2026-10-24-0208-engine-frame-plan`,
`docs/debt/s3-frame-cost-scales-per-fixture.md`). These are the tools that
measure `b`.

## The lane projects

`gen_lanes_project.py <out> <lanes> <leds per lane> [shader.glsl]` writes
skirt-620's shape scaled: one clock, one shader (skirt-620's psrdnoise noise),
and per lane one 8-column Map2d grid fixture (Direct sampling at 32 × 32)
feeding one WS281x output on the next XIAO pad. The `projects/test/lanes-*`
were generated by it; regenerate them rather than editing by hand.

| project | lanes × LEDs | for |
|---|---|---|
| `lanes-2x384` | 2 × 384 = 768 | the emulated differential (A) and its heap-budget record |
| `lanes-6x128` | 6 × 128 = 768 | the emulated differential (B) and its heap-budget record |
| `lanes-2x512` | 2 × 512 = 1,024 | the host allocation test (A) and the silicon fit |
| `lanes-8x128` | 8 × 128 = 1,024 | the host allocation test (B) and the silicon fit |
| `lanes-6x248` | 6 × 248 = 1,488 | silicon: the fps report's "1,488" (its `n6x248`) |
| `lanes-8x256` | 8 × 256 = 2,048 | silicon: the 2,048 row |

Same LEDs, different lanes, is the differential: the LED term cancels and
what is left is per lane. The 8-lane projects do not fit the RV32 engine
emulator's 320 KiB heap (each lane retains ~10 KB at load, and the compile
that follows runs out), so the emulated pair is 2 × 384 against 6 × 128.

The fps report's other two sizes are `projects/test/skirt-620` (2 × 310 =
620) and, for 1,488, `lanes-6x248`. `projects/test/zook-dome-1500` is a
different shape (one fixture, one output, 1,500 lamps) and is not a lane
measurement.

## What measures it

- `per-lane-cost.sh`: `lp-cli profile` steady render of the emulated pair;
  prints cycles and allocations per lane and the fixed remainder, with the
  commit. Emulated cycles are a direction, never a device number.
- `lpc-engine`'s `steady_frame_allocations_do_not_scale_with_lanes`
  (`src/engine/steady_frame_alloc_tests.rs`): the same differential on the
  host's real nodes, a ratchet on allocations per lane.
- The heap-budget gate (`just heap-budget-check`) records `lanes-2x384` and
  `lanes-6x128`, so CI prices the per-lane allocations and bytes.
- `lpc-engine/tests/frame_plan_differential.rs`: not a cost, the correctness
  oracle the frame plans are held to (every corpus project, every kind of
  edit, every mode byte-identical).
- Silicon: `[stage]` / `[perf]` on FS3 at the sizes above, fitted for `b`.
