Provenance: esp32c6-bootloader-idf-v5.1.bin and esp32c6-bootloader-idf-v5.1-quiet.bin

What they are
  esp32c6-bootloader-idf-v5.1.bin (SHIPPED: the esp32c6-4mb build def names
  it) is the ESP32-C6 ESP-IDF second-stage bootloader espflash 3.3.0 bundles
  — the one every LightPlayer C6 has carried — rebuilt from source. It is
  byte-identical to espflash 3.3.0's resources/bootloaders/esp32c6-bootloader.bin.

  esp32c6-bootloader-idf-v5.1-quiet.bin (NOT shipped; a fallback) is the
  same build with one change: CONFIG_BOOTLOADER_LOG_LEVEL_NONE=y. It prints
  nothing on UART0 during a good boot (the stock one prints ~22 lines at
  115,200 baud, ~145 ms of every boot). An assert or abort() still prints:
  those go through esp_rom_printf, not the log. Booted on the emulator only.

Reproducibility
  On arm64 (Docker 29, M2 Max), stock and quiet each built twice: the same
  sha256 every time, and stock equal to espflash's binary. The x64 run is the
  `Bootloader reproducible` workflow (.github/workflows/bootloader-reproducible.yml).

Image headers (before a flasher patches flash mode/size/frequency into bytes
2-3 and recomputes the appended SHA-256, as espflash does at merge)
  chip id 13 (ESP32-C6), chip revision v0.0..v0.99, hash appended
  stock:  seg 0x4086c410 len 0xd48 (data), 0x4086e610 len 0x2d68 (code),
          0x40875720 len 0x1800 (code)
  quiet:  seg 0x4086c410 len 0xa98 (data), 0x4086e610 len 0x2404 (code),
          0x40875720 len 0x4e8 (code)
  The quiet code ranges lie inside the stock ones, the ranges Studio's
  hung-bootloader detection reads a ROM `Saved PC` against
  (lpa_devices::bootloader).
